1. Login:
   $ vercel login

2. Deploy:
   $ vercel

3. Follow prompts (answer Y, N, ramsider, ./, N)

4. Done! Your site is live at: https://ramsider-xxx.vercel.app

⏱️  Total time: ~2 minutes


┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ METHOD 2: Website (Visual)                                   │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

1. Go to: https://vercel.com/new

2. Click "Continue with GitHub"

3. Import your "ramsider" repository

4. Click "Deploy" (keep all defaults)

5. Done! Your site is live!

⏱️  Total time: ~2 minutes
