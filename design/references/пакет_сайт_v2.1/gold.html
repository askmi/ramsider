02 · Technology

What it does

Three-level heating and a grill function create full-bodied “3D” smoke, preserving every note of aroma and flavour. The tobacco is baked, not burned — without charcoal.

The smoke always stays cool. The system monitors water temperature and level, and warns you if the water overheats.

Choose Light, Medium or Strong. Advanced mode offers top, bottom and grill settings, with Turbo OFF, DIRECT or SMART.

Your personal account is coming soon. Basic operation starts from the device screen without a phone. Scan the QR code on a branded capsule with your phone.
