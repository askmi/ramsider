PRIVACY POLICY

of the online store of RAMSIDER SMOKING & TOBACCO HEATING DEVICES TRADING CO. L.L.C. (UAE)

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") explains how RAMSIDER SMOKING & TOBACCO HEATING DEVICES TRADING CO. L.L.C. (Trade License No. 1050925, Dubai, United Arab Emirates) ("RAMSIDER", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal data of users of the online store at www.ramsider.com (the "Website").

This Policy is issued in accordance with Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (the "PDPL"), Federal Decree-Law No. 14 of 2023 on Trading by Modern Technology Means, Federal Law No. 15 of 2020 on Consumer Protection and other applicable laws of the United Arab Emirates ("UAE"). For the purposes of the PDPL, RAMSIDER is the Controller of your personal data.

This Policy applies to the Website and to orders placed through it, and to communications with our consultant bot in Telegram (@RamsiderConsultantBot), in WhatsApp and with the official RAMSIDER accounts on Instagram. It does not apply to third-party websites linked from the Website.

1. DEFINITIONS

Personal Data — any data relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

Processing — any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, recording, organisation, storage, adaptation, retrieval, use, disclosure, transfer, restriction, erasure and destruction, whether or not by automated means.

Controller — RAMSIDER, which determines the purposes and means of processing.

Processor — a third party that processes Personal Data on our behalf and under our instructions (for example, delivery companies, payment service providers, hosting providers).

User or you — a natural person who visits the Website, places an order or contacts us via the consultant bot, messengers or social media.

Consent — a specific, clear and unambiguous indication of your wishes, given by a clear affirmative action (for example, ticking a box), by which you agree to the processing of your Personal Data.

Data Office — the UAE Data Office, the federal authority supervising compliance with the PDPL.

Cookies — small text files stored on your device when you visit the Website.

2. PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT

2.1. Data you provide to us:

full name;

mobile phone number;

email address;

delivery address (emirate, city, area, street, building, apartment/villa);

confirmation that you are 18 years of age or older and, where required, your date of birth;

order details: products ordered, order history, communications with our customer service;

account login and password (if you create an account).

2.2. Data collected automatically when you use the Website: IP address, device and browser type, operating system, language settings, pages visited, date and time of access, referring page, and Cookie data (see Section 5).

2.3. Payment card data is entered directly on the secure page of our payment service provider or acquiring bank and is processed by them under the PCI DSS standard. We do not receive or store your full card number or CVV; we only receive confirmation of the payment and limited transaction details (for example, the last four digits of the card).

2.4. We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data (such as health, biometric, religious or criminal-record data). Please do not provide such data through the Website.

2.5. We collect only the data that is necessary for the purposes set out in Section 3. You must not provide personal data of other persons without a lawful basis (for example, when ordering a gift, only the recipient's name, phone and address).

4. AGE RESTRICTION

4.1. The Website and the products sold on it are intended exclusively for adults aged 18 and over. Sale of these products to persons under 18 is prohibited in the UAE.

4.2. Before placing an order you must confirm that you are at least 18 years old. The courier may ask you to present a valid identity document (Emirates ID or passport) on delivery to verify your age. The courier only checks the document visually; we do not copy or store it.

4.3. We do not knowingly collect Personal Data of persons under 18. If we learn that we have collected such data, we will cancel the order and delete the data without undue delay, except where retention is required by law.

5. COOKIES

5.1. The Website uses:

strictly necessary Cookies — required for the Website to function (shopping cart, login, security, remembering your Cookie choices). They do not require consent and cannot be disabled;

marketing Cookies — used to show relevant advertising, if applicable.

5.2. Marketing Cookies are placed only after you give consent through the Cookie banner on your first visit. You may change your choice at any time via the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of the Website or in your browser settings. Disabling Cookies may affect some Website functions.

6. SHARING OF PERSONAL DATA AND TRANSFERS OUTSIDE THE UAE

6.1. We do not sell, rent or trade your Personal Data. We share it only with the following recipients, and only to the extent necessary:

delivery and courier companies, including international carriers — to deliver your order in the UAE and abroad and to verify your age;

our payment service provider and acquirer, Network International — to process payments and refunds;

Anthropic (Claude model) — to prepare replies of our consultant bot with the help of artificial intelligence;

hosting, IT, email and messaging service providers (WhatsApp, Telegram) — to operate the Website and communicate with you;

advertising providers — only if you have consented to the relevant Cookies;

professional advisers (auditors, lawyers, accountants) — under a duty of confidentiality;

competent UAE government, judicial and law-enforcement authorities — where required by law.

6.2. Our Processors act only on our documented instructions under written agreements that require them to keep your data confidential and secure and to use it solely for the agreed purposes.

6.3. To deliver orders, we transfer the Personal Data needed for delivery (name, phone number, email, delivery address, order details) to courier and delivery companies outside the UAE and their partners in the country of destination. Our email and messaging service providers (WhatsApp, Telegram), Instagram and the artificial intelligence provider Anthropic (USA) may also process your Personal Data outside the UAE. Such transfers are made in accordance with Articles 22 and 23 of the PDPL and are necessary to perform our contract with you.

7. RETENTION OF PERSONAL DATA

7.1. We keep Personal Data only for as long as necessary for the purposes in Section 3:

order, invoice and payment records — for the periods required by UAE tax laws (5 years for VAT records and 7 years for corporate tax records, from the end of the relevant tax period);

account data — until you delete your account or after 3 years of inactivity;

data processed on the basis of consent (marketing, non-essential Cookies) — until you withdraw consent or after 3 years of inactivity, whichever is earlier;

email correspondence with customer service — 3 years from the last contact;

correspondence with our consultant bot, in WhatsApp and on Instagram — up to 90 days (see Section 11).

7.2. After the retention period expires, Personal Data is securely deleted or anonymised so that you can no longer be identified.

8. SECURITY AND DATA BREACHES

8.1. We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Personal Data against unauthorised or accidental access, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction, including:

encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connection to the Website;

payment processing by PCI DSS-certified providers;

access to Personal Data limited to authorised staff and Processors who need it for their duties;

confidentiality obligations of employees and Processors;

regular review of our security measures and keeping a record of processing activities.

8.2. If a breach affects the privacy, confidentiality or security of your Personal Data, we will notify the UAE Data Office immediately upon becoming aware of it, in accordance with Article 9 of the PDPL. Where the breach may prejudice your privacy, confidentiality or security, we will also notify you and inform you of the measures taken.

8.3. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. Please keep your account password confidential and contact us immediately if you suspect unauthorised use of your account.

9. YOUR RIGHTS

9.1. Under the PDPL (Articles 6, 13–18 and 24), you have the right to:

access — obtain, free of charge, information on the types of Personal Data we process, the purposes of processing, decisions made by automated processing (including profiling), the recipients inside and outside the UAE, the retention period, how to request rectification, erasure, restriction of or objection to processing, the protection measures for cross-border transfers, the measures taken in case of a data breach, and how to file a complaint;

data portability — where processing is based on your consent or a contract with you and is carried out by automated means, receive the Personal Data you provided to us in a structured, machine-readable format and, where technically feasible, ask us to transfer it to another controller;

rectification — have inaccurate Personal Data corrected or incomplete Personal Data completed without undue delay;

erasure — have your Personal Data deleted where it is no longer necessary, you withdraw consent, you object and there is no legitimate ground to continue, or the processing is unlawful;

restriction — have processing restricted while you contest the accuracy of the data, or where processing goes beyond the agreed purposes or is unlawful; you may also ask us to keep your data after the purpose of processing has been achieved if you need it to establish or defend legal claims;

objection — object to and stop processing for direct marketing, including related profiling, and for statistical surveys;

automated decisions — object to decisions based solely on automated processing (including profiling) that produce legal or similarly serious effects on you, and request human review of such decisions. The right to object does not apply where such processing is provided for in a contract with you, is required by law, or you have given prior consent to it;

withdraw consent — at any time, as easily as you gave it; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it;

complain — submit a complaint to the UAE Data Office if you believe your Personal Data is processed in breach of the PDPL.

9.2. To exercise your rights, email us at support@ramsider.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on your request; if you write from an email address not linked to your order or account, we will verify your identity in another secure way (for example, using your order number and the phone number used for the order). We will respond within 30 days of receiving the request, or within any shorter period set by the PDPL Executive Regulations. Requests are free of charge. We may refuse a request that is unrelated, excessively repetitive or may affect the rights of third parties.

9.3. We may refuse erasure or restriction of processing, in whole or in part, where the law requires us to keep the data (for example, invoices required for tax purposes) or the data is needed for an investigation or to establish or defend legal claims. In that case we will explain the reasons.

10. MARKETING COMMUNICATIONS

10.1. We send marketing messages by email/WhatsApp/Telegram only to adult Users who have given separate consent by ticking an unticked box. We do not send marketing messages by SMS. Consent to marketing is not a condition of placing an order.

10.2. You can unsubscribe at any time in your personal account on the Website, using the "unsubscribe" link in any email, or by emailing support@ramsider.com. Service messages about your order (confirmation, delivery status) are not marketing and will still be sent.

11. CONSULTANT BOT, MESSENGERS AND SOCIAL MEDIA

11.1. When you write to our consultant bot in Telegram (@RamsiderConsultantBot), contact us via WhatsApp or contact the official RAMSIDER accounts on Instagram (comments and direct messages), we process your user ID and username on the relevant platform, the text of your messages and any contact details you provide.

11.2. Replies may be prepared with the help of artificial intelligence. For this purpose, the text of your message is transferred to our processor, Anthropic (Claude model, USA), solely to prepare a reply. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models. Please do not share health information, identity document details or payment card details in these chats.

11.3. We do not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you based solely on automated processing.

11.4. We keep correspondence with the bot, in WhatsApp and on Instagram for up to 90 days and then delete it. You can request earlier deletion by emailing support@ramsider.com. Where a conversation relates to an order, the order details are kept for the periods set out in Section 7.

11.5. Telegram, WhatsApp and Instagram process your messages as independent controllers in accordance with their own privacy policies.
