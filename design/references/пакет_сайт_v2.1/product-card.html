Technical specifications

Dimensions
128 × 128 × 300 mm
Weight
2.755 kg with battery, excluding hose, mouthpiece and connector. Battery: 650 g.
Warm-up
6 minutes
Battery life
Approximately 1.5 hours of an active session; up to 2.5 hours in Sleep Mode with pauses.
Charging
Full charge: 1.5–2 hours. Charging temperature: 0–45 °C.
Battery
Removable Li-ion UNO-FX05: 21.6 V, 6,400 mAh, 138.24 Wh. No hot swapping. Mains operation is possible with the original power supply.
Heating
Three controlled zones (HeatCore™), with water and temperature monitoring.
Modes
Light / Medium / Strong; advanced settings for upper / lower / grill heaters; Turbo OFF / DIRECT / SMART.

Operating conditions
0–30 °C. The housing is not waterproof. 2–3 sessions per day.
