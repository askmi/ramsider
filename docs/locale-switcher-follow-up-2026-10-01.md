## Scope and source

The user clarified the approved three-state [locale reference](../design/references/locale-switcher/README.md): the open list should show page artwork through a neutral translucent surface with a fine gold rim, and the top desktop capsule should share one visible line with RAMSIDER and the menu bars. After scroll the capsule may move to the viewport edge. These are [CHG-0036](../CHANGELOG.md#chg-0036) and [CHG-0037](../CHANGELOG.md#chg-0037). The user-provided desktop screenshot is preserved locally at `screenshots/source/locale-header-desktop-user-2026-09-30.png` (SHA-256 `56b115fa…54214e`). The original product art and copy have not been changed by this follow-up.

## Mapping and final UI

The 941px source canvas puts the header's optical center near source Y80. At scrollY=0, `app/globals.css` gives the 44px locale hit box `top: 80/941 × min(viewport width, 941px) − 22px` with a 4px/safe-area minimum. The 44px menu summary uses a matching scaled margin, so its painted bars, capsule face and wordmark center stay within 3 CSS px in tested widths. The scrolled control has a separate 12px/safe-area top offset. The panel's maximum height subtracts the actual header offset and leaves a 12px bottom gap; on 1440×280 its box is `y=98`, `height=170`, bottom `268` and the final Korean option is reachable by internal scroll.

The earlier 144px neutral graphite panel (alpha 0.68, white labels, selected check) was later rejected by the user. The current 112px panel is light `rgb(250 247 242 / .45)` with blur 4px, a thin gold border, dark labels, and translucent selected/hover states. The check and its reserved space are removed; eleven flag/name pairs remain on one line. Alpha 0.45 is a further transparency increase from the intermediate light alpha 0.64 that the user rejected. Artwork and page copy show through the whole panel. Headless WebKit screenshots did not visibly blur background glyphs even with a diagnostic 100px `backdrop-filter`, so legibility does not depend on that effect.

## Current light-panel verification

The first v6 Playwright invocation failed all 36 cases before page load because the sandbox aborted browser processes. The permitted production rerun [passed 16 applicable tests with 20 profile-specific skips and no failures](../screenshots/actual/locale-switcher/light-2026-10-01-v6/playwright-production-escalated.log). The independent visual auditor passed the nine current captures after explicitly comparing with rejected alpha 0.64 and prior dark alpha 0.68. In dark screenshots, sampled row backgrounds against the declared `#080503` label color were approximately 4.74–5.09:1 on Pro and 4.85–5.18:1 on Pro Max; this sample is not a full WCAG certification. The `:3020` production preview was rebuilt with the light alpha 0.45 CSS and refreshed in the open browser tab. User acceptance has not been recorded.

The [current loopback performance sample](../screenshots/actual/locale-switcher/light-2026-10-01-v6/performance.json) at 402×874/DPR3 recorded FCP/LCP 96ms, CLS 0, 153,707B JavaScript transfer, 1,165,783B total initial resource transfer and open-menu scroll p95 16.8ms with no frame above 33ms in 89 measured frames. INP and Lighthouse were not measured. Loopback timing is not field performance.
